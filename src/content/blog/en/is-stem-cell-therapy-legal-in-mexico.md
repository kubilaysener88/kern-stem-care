## Short answer

Regenerative and cellular therapies in Mexico are regulated by **COFEPRIS** (the federal
health authority). Clinics, physicians and cellular-therapy laboratories must be licensed.
We only connect patients with COFEPRIS-licensed providers. <span class="ph">[Expand with specifics.]</span>

## What "investigational" means

Many regenerative therapies are **not approved by the U.S. FDA or Health Canada** for
specific diseases and are considered investigational. Outcomes vary and no result is
guaranteed. <span class="ph">[Explain the evidence/regulatory status you communicate to patients.]</span>

## Questions to ask before you travel

- Is the clinic and its laboratory **COFEPRIS-licensed**? Can they share credentials?
- Who performs the medical evaluation, and what does it include?
- What is the written, itemized quote — and who do you pay?
